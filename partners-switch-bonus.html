*New Qualified Client — a new client directly referred by you who, within the same monthly performance period, has their STARTRADER trading account approved, completes a minimum Net Deposit of USD 500, and completes at least 2 standard lots of closed trades in FX pairs, Oil, Gold, Silver or BTCUSD. †Total Net Deposit — eligible deposits from your directly referred clients less their eligible withdrawals in the same period. A negative Net Deposit is carried forward and deducted from the following period(s).

‡Total Notional Volume — eligible closed trading volume from your directly referred clients in the same period. Open positions are not counted.

Only clients directly referred under your partner account count. Sub-IB and sub-affiliate activity does not count unless approved by STARTRADER. Full definitions are set out in the Terms and Conditions.
