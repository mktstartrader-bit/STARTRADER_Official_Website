FAQ

Trading app questions

Which platforms does the STARTRADER app support?
The app supports both MT4 and MT5, so you get comprehensive functionality and seamless trading on either platform from the same download. It runs on iOS and Android, on phones and tablets.
What can I trade on the app?
Over 200 products, including forex, bonds, metals, indices, commodities and shares. The same instruments available on desktop are available on mobile.
Which order types are available?
Market, limit, stop-loss and trailing stop orders. Risk management tools are built in, so you can set your exits when you open the position rather than afterwards.
Which account types work with the app?
STP, ECN and Demo accounts all work with the app. Commission-free trading is available on the applicable account types, and leverage of up to 1:1000 is available subject to your account and jurisdiction.
Can I deposit and withdraw from the app?
Yes. Multiple deposit and withdrawal methods are supported, with zero deposit fees on most methods. Processing times and available methods depend on your region and the entity your account is registered with.
Is the app free to use?
Yes, the app is free to download and use. Trading costs are the spread and, on commission-based accounts, the commission per lot. Those are set out on the trading account page.
Do I need an account before I download it?
No. You can download the app and open a demo account inside it to practise with virtual funds on live pricing before you fund anything.
Does leverage make the app riskier?
Leverage amplifies both profits and losses regardless of the device you trade on. Leveraged products carry a high level of risk and can cost more than your initial deposit, so use the risk management tools and size positions deliberately.
