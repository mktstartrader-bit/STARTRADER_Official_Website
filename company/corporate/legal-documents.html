FAQ

Legal document questions

Which entity's documents apply to me?
The entity your account is registered with, which is named in the client agreement you accepted and on your account statements. Each STARTRADER entity, its regulator and its licence number are listed on the regulation page.
Do I have to read all of these?
The Client Agreement and the Risk Disclosure are the two that govern your relationship with us and the risks you are taking, so read those. The others apply when relevant to you, such as the Swap-Free Accounts Policy if you hold that account type.
What happens if a document is updated?
Material changes to the terms are notified to clients, and the current version is always the one published here. If you keep trading after a change takes effect, the updated terms apply to your account.
How do I raise a complaint?
Email support@startrader.com with Complaint in the subject line and include your account number. The Complaints Procedure Policy sets out the process. If the outcome does not resolve the matter, you can escalate to the Financial Commission.
Where can I check the regulatory details?
The regulation page lists every STARTRADER entity with its regulator, licence number and registered office, and links to each regulator's own public register so you can verify the licence independently.
Are these documents available in other languages?
Ask support which translations are available for your entity. Where a translation exists, the English version normally prevails in the event of any inconsistency, and the document itself will say so.
