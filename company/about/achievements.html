About the awards

How many awards has STARTRADER won?
Fourteen at the last count, across execution, spreads, trading technology, platform and copy trading — listed in full above with the titles as each awarding body presented them.
What do the execution awards actually measure?
Fill quality and spread consistency under real conditions rather than marketing reach. That is why they matter more to a trader than a brand award does.
Are awards a guarantee of results?
No. An award describes conditions and service at a point in time. It says nothing about what any individual position will do, and past performance is never a guide to future results.
Where can I read about each one?
Each award is announced as it is received. The announcements page carries the detail, and the media coverage page carries what was written about it.
