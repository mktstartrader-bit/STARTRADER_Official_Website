FAQ

About the congress

What is the MENA Investment Congress?
A regional investment forum organised by CFA Society Emirates. The 2025 edition — its seventh — brought together more than 500 participants and over 30 speakers in Dubai to connect regional markets with global capital flows.
What was STARTRADER’s role?
STARTRADER was a partner of the 2025 congress. The point of being there was the conversation: what institutional and retail participants in the region actually need next from a broker.
Why does a broker attend a policy forum?
Because platform decisions get better when they are informed by the people making allocation decisions. A congress puts investors, policymakers and technologists in one room, which a product roadmap cannot do on its own.
Does the partnership affect trading conditions?
No. Spreads, commissions and execution are set by your account type and by market conditions, and are unaffected by any partnership.
