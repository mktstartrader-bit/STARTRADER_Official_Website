FAQ

Questions about STARCARES

What is STARCARES?
STARCARES is the name we give our corporate social responsibility work — community infrastructure, disaster relief, education and youth leadership, run through the regional teams rather than from head office.
How are projects chosen?
Local teams propose them. The test is whether a company our size can change something specific and then keep it maintained, rather than fund a one-off that degrades within a year.
Can my organisation apply for support?
Yes. Contact the regional office nearest you through the contact page and it will reach the team that reviews proposals for that market.
Where do you operate?
Across the markets our licensed entities cover — the UAE, Australia, South Africa, Mauritius and Seychelles among them. CSR work follows that footprint.
