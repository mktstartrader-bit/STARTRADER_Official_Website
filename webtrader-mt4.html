FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does MT4 have a web platform?
Yes. MT4 WebTrader is the browser version of MetaTrader 4 — the same account, instruments and analysis tools as the desktop terminal, reached from any machine without installing anything.
Can I use MT4 in a browser?
Yes, on any current Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari. The web terminal takes no consideration of the operating system, so it runs the same on Windows, macOS and Linux.
How do I use MT4 WebTrader?
Sign in with your MetaTrader 4 credentials, find the instrument in Market Watch, open the chart, then place the order from the chart or the order ticket. Everything is on one screen.
How do I log into MT4 Web?
Open the browser, go to the web terminal, and enter the login, password and server shown on your MetaTrader 4 account. It is the same process as the desktop terminal.
What are the system requirements?
A current browser and a stable connection. There is no additional software to install, and performance varies mainly with the network rather than the machine.
Can I trade all instruments on MT4 WebTrader?
Yes. Forex majors, minors and exotics, commodities in metals and energies, indices, share CFDs and cryptocurrencies where they are available to your account.
How does it compare to the desktop version?
Function is close to identical. The desktop terminal holds heavier custom tooling such as expert advisors and keeps working offline for chart study; the browser starts in seconds and needs nothing installed.
Is MT4 WebTrader secure?
Sessions are encrypted in transit and client funds are held in segregated accounts. Use a network you trust, and do not save credentials on a machine you share.
Can I use it on my mobile device?
Yes. It is responsive down to a phone, though on a phone the STARTRADER app is the more comfortable experience. Both read the same account.
What are the steps to set it up the first time?
Open a live account, choose MetaTrader 4 as the platform, fund the account, then sign in to WebTrader with the credentials you were issued. Nothing else is needed.
