MT5 Mobile App: How To Trade Forex On Your Phone

Download the MT5 mobile app for Android and trade global markets on the go. Learn setup, features, adding XAUUSD, placing trades and setting alerts.

STARTRADER Research DeskPublished 2 August 2026 · Updated 13 August 2026 · 13 min read
Link copied

You can monitor global markets, place orders and control your open positions right from your phone with the MT5 mobile app.

But do you know that while trading on the go is incredibly convenient, it is purposefully designed to work in conjunction with your desktop platform?

Android is considered to be the most used device by Indian retail traders to access the financial markets. Many traders use the MT5 mobile application on a daily basis to monitor live quotes, view price charts, control open positions and manage their trading accounts.

The app offers all the tools needed for trade monitoring and simple management from any spot with an internet connection. But for advanced charting, custom indicator analysis and automated trading capabilities desktop platforms are still the best bet.

This complete guide will tell you all you need to know about mobile trading, from downloading the platform to understanding the interface, adding instruments, placing trades, and navigating mobile limitations.

Download MT5 Mobile Android: How To Install The App

Android users can easily download the official platform from the app store and connect it to a broker account in no time.

It takes just minutes to install the app on your smartphone. Android is an open and very accessible operating system, which makes it easy to get the platform up and running.

Step-By-Step Android Download

Below are the practical steps to install the application:

  • Open up the Google Play Store on your Android phone.
  • Type “MetaTrader 5” in the top search bar.
  • Make sure the app is the official one published by MetaQuotes.
  • Click the install button, and wait for the download to complete
  • Once installed, the app can be launched immediately.
  • In the setup menu, find your broker’s server (such as the STARTRADER server).
  • Securely log in with your existing demo or live account credentials.

Is The App Free?

The application is completely free to download and install from the Google Play Store. However, if you want to trade live, you will need to open an account with a broker and they will charge you the usual trading fees or spreads.

Android Setup Notes

When setting up your mobile trading environment, don’t forget to consider these key technical points:

  • Always ensure you have a stable internet connection so that the price quotes update accurately.
  • To take advantage of security updates, keep the application updated to the latest version.
  • If you want to use price alerts or get account updates, turn on device notifications.
  • First, open a practice demo account to get familiar with the interface before you start trading real money.

MT5 Mobile App Interface: What You Need To Know

The mobile interface is intuitively arranged in dedicated tabs for Quotes, Charts, Trade, History and Messages for easy navigation.

To be an efficient trader, you need to know how to navigate the platform. Data shows that retail participation via mobile devices continues to grow rapidly in emerging markets, making mobile interface literacy a critical skill.

Quotes Tab

The Quotes tab will give you a live list of your selected instruments with real-time bid and ask prices and the current spreads. This is the main dashboard where users can monitor market movement, select symbols and initiate new trade actions.

Charts Tab

In the Charts tab you can see the history and live price movements of any instrument you select. From this screen, users can easily change the timeframes, change the chart types and apply basic technical indicators. For a deeper dive into how to read these price movements, check out our in-depth MT5 charts guide here.

Trade Tab

In the Trade tab you have a real-time overview of all your open positions and pending orders. It shows important account parameters such as your current account balance, active equity, used margin, free margin, floating profit or loss.

History Tab

The History tab is like your personal ledger, showing you a complete record of your previous trades and closed positions. Users can also view their account deposit history, withdrawal records and general account activity for custom time periods.

Messages Tab

The Messages section is a dedicated communication hub for the platform. It shows important push-notifications, secure account messages and relevant platform-related updates from your broker or MetaQuotes.

How To Add XAUUSD On MT5 Mobile App

If the connected broker offers the symbol, users can easily add instruments like XAUUSD from the Quotes tab.

Customizing your watchlist means you’ll only see the markets that matter to your trading routine. Gold is a very popular market for many Indian retail traders and knowing how to find and show it is important.

Step-By-Step Instrument Setup

Here’s a practical process to populate your watchlist:

  • Launch the app and tap on the Quotes tab at the bottom of the screen.
  • Select the plus (+) icon or search for symbols at the top right.
  • Search for XAUUSD or enter the exact symbol format your broker uses.
  • Select the instrument from the populated list available.
  • Add to your Quotes dashboard.
  • Tap the new symbol to open its chart or see trade options available.

Why XAUUSD May Not Appear Immediately

Note: Instrument names and ticker symbols may differ slightly depending on the broker. Some platforms will use XAUUSD, but others will just list the asset as Gold, GOLD or add a unique suffix to it. If you don’t find the exact symbol in the search, you can browse manually through the available folders like Metals, Commodities or CFD.

Pinning Or Organising Instruments

On the Quotes tab, users can add the most frequently used symbols and delete the unused symbols, so that they can arrange their instruments in a precise way. Drag and drop symbols to reorder them by tapping the pencil icon, and pin your most monitored markets to the top for easy access.

How To Place A Trade On MT5 Mobile

Users can place instant market orders and also pending orders directly from the Quotes, Chart, or Trade sections via the platform.

It is a precise act to execute on a smartphone. The application makes this simpler by giving you a clear, easy-to-use order window that lists all the important trade parameters before the trade is executed.

Market Orders

A market order is an order to enter a position immediately at the available price in the market.

If you want to place a market order, follow these steps:

  • Go to the Quotes tab and choose the market you want.
  • Tap the instrument you want to swap.
  • Select either “Trade” or “New Order” from the pop-up menu.
  • Select the order type as “Market Execution”.
  • Specify the size of the lot you want to trade.
  • Confirm the symbol, current price and order details are correct.
  • When you want to place the order, press the “Buy” or “Sell” button.

Pending Orders

Pending orders are orders to open a trade automatically when the market price reaches a certain level in the future. Buy Limits, Sell Limits, Buy Stops and Sell Stops are not to be trusted in live market conditions until beginners have taken the time to fully understand the differences.

How To Set TP And SL On MT5 Mobile

When you place an order you can set take profit and stop loss levels. You can also change an open position later.

Managing downside risk and securing potential profits are the two basic elements of trade management. Mobile users enjoy full access to these types of protective orders.

A trader could open a buy position on XAUUSD at 2300.00 and decide to put a stop loss at 2290.00 to limit their maximum risk of loss, and set a take profit at 2320.00 to automatically close the trade if the market moves up to that level.

During Order Placement

  • Open a new trading window for the instrument you selected.
  • Enter the desired trade volume.
  • Tap on the “Stop Loss” field and enter your risk price level.
  • Click on the “Take Profit” box and input your desired price level.
  • Review the order parameters.
  • Execute the trade.

After A Position Is Open

  • Click on the Trade tab at the bottom of your screen.
  • Press and hold the specific open position you want to edit.
  • Select Modify Position from the context menu.
  • Add or change the exact stop loss and take profit values.
  • Confirm and save changes.

Note: This guide does not provide specific take profit levels, stop loss levels, trade sizes or price targets. Always be aware of market volatility and practice tight risk management when placing protective orders.

How To Set Price Alert On MT5 Mobile

You can set the app to send you push notifications when a market reaches a certain level depending on your device settings and the version of the platform you are using.

You don’t have to sit at a screen all day to keep up with fast-moving markets. Real-time notifications are critical to today’s retail trading infrastructure as they enable users to react to volatility quickly.

What Price Alerts Do

Price alerts allow users to be instantly notified when a chosen market condition or price point is reached. The automation feature lets traders monitor the global instruments on the move, without having to constantly watch the mobile chart.

Mobile Notification Setup

Users must check the following carefully to make sure that alerts work correctly:

  • App notification permissions in their general Android settings.
  • Internal notification settings within the app itself.
  • How stable their mobile internet connection is.
  • Their active account login status.
  • Device battery restrictions can pause background app activity.

Price Alert Setup Direction

To set up an alert natively on your device:

  • Open the instrument you need from the Quotes or Charts tab.
  • In newer versions of the app, there will be an alert or notification icon (usually a bell).
  • Set the price condition and bid/ask parameter, if supported.
  • Confirm the alert and save.
  • Ensure that notifications for the app are enabled in your Android operating system.

Note: Some alert workflows are best done on the desktop platform and then pushed to the mobile app via a MetaQuotes ID. Users should follow the specific setup options available in their app and the broker platform version to ensure notifications sync properly across devices.

MT5 Mobile Vs Desktop: What Can’t You Do On Mobile?

The mobile version is good for monitoring on the move but it lacks the advanced customisation options, automated strategy execution or multi-chart workspace functionality of the desktop platform.

Mobile apps are designed for speed and ease. However, professional technical analysis often requires the power of processing and screen real estate that only a desktop setup can provide. If you want to do deep analysis then you should download MT5 on PC to supplement your mobile trading.

Expert Advisors

Expert Advisors are automated trading scripts that are generally only installed, compiled and maintained on the desktop platform. You cannot run or configure these automated systems directly from your Android app. For more information on automation, see our Expert Advisors guide.

Custom Indicators

The Android application offers a decent selection of built-in technical indicators, but you generally can’t install custom desktop indicator files (.mq5 or .ex5) directly into the mobile app. Desktop software is still the best place to do custom indicator work.

Strategy Tester

The Strategy Tester is a powerful desktop feature. It is used specifically for back testing Expert Advisors and evaluating automated strategies using historical data. Mobile users should not expect to see all Strategy Tester capabilities on their smartphones.

Multi-Chart And Workspace Limits

Mobile screens are smaller by design. This makes handling complex multi-chart analysis, very detailed chart layouts and sophisticated workspace management on a desktop monitor much easier.

Conclusion

The platform is a great bridge to the markets enabling traders to check quotes, read charts, place trades and modify parameters like stop losses right from an Android device.

The mobile app is still free to download and is an excellent companion tool for checking daily quotes, analyzing simple charts, adding essential instruments like XAUUSD and actively monitoring open positions.

Once you get familiar with the core tabs Quotes, Charts, Trade, History and Messages, navigating the application is a breeze. Users can easily place trades and set protective orders, but setting price alerts may depend heavily on app and desktop push notification settings, as well as internal device permissions.

Finally, the mobile application offers complete freedom to manage accounts remotely, but the desktop software remains the best choice for running Expert Advisors, installing custom indicators, using the Strategy Tester, and conducting detailed technical analysis.
