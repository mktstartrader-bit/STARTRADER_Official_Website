FAQ

Before you get in touch

How quickly will I get a reply?
Live chat is answered around the clock, usually in under a minute. Emails to support@startrader.com are answered within two hours, and messages sent through the form on this page go straight to the desk you select, so they aren’t passed between teams.
Is support really available 24 hours a day?
Yes. Live chat and email are staffed 24 hours a day, five days a week while markets are open, with cover over the weekend for account and funding questions. Support is available in more than twenty languages.
What should I include when I contact support about my account?
Include your trading account number, the platform you were using, and the time of the event with your timezone. For an order or execution question, add the order ticket number. That’s usually enough for the first reply to resolve it rather than ask for more detail.
How do I raise a complaint?
Email support@startrader.com with “Complaint” in the subject line and include your account number and a description of what happened. Complaints are logged and escalated to the compliance team of the entity your account is registered with, and you’ll receive written acknowledgement.
Which office is my account with?
You’re assigned to a STARTRADER entity based on your country of residence when you register, and the entity is named in your account documents. Every entity, its regulator, licence number and registered office address are set out in the regulatory information at the foot of this page.
Can I visit an office in person?
Our offices are corporate premises rather than retail branches, so account servicing is handled online or by phone. If you need to meet the team, arrange it in advance through support and we’ll confirm whether a meeting is possible at that location.
Where do partnership and institutional enquiries go?
Introducing broker, affiliate, money manager and institutional enquiries are handled by the partnerships desk at partners@startrader.com, or select Partnerships on the form on this page and it’s routed there.
Do you offer support in my language?
Support is provided in more than twenty languages including English, Arabic, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Malay, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi and Turkish. Choose your language on the form and we’ll reply in it where possible.
