FAQ

STAR Synthetic questions

What are STAR Synthetic Indices?
Algorithm-generated markets that simulate real price behaviour and run 24/7. Because prices come from fair algorithms rather than world events, there are no news gaps, no closed sessions and no weekend breaks.
Are the prices fair?
Yes. Every index is generated by a transparent algorithm that no one — including STARTRADER — can predict or influence, and it is unaffected by outside events.
When can I trade them?
Around the clock, every day of the year. Synthetic markets do not close.
What do I need to start?
A STAR Synthetic account and as little as $0.50. Leverage is available up to 1:10000 — remember that leverage amplifies losses as well as profits.
Which series should I choose?
ConstaVol for steady, constant volatility; Boost/Dip if you want event-style spikes and drops; PriceStep for fixed-size moves suited to scalping and EA testing.
