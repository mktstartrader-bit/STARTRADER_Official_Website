Important information

Good to know before you transact

A few practical details so your deposits and withdrawals always go through smoothly.

Withdrawal forms received after 21:00 (GMT) are processed the following working day.

Funds can only be returned to a bank account in the same name (or joint) as your STARTRADER account.

International Telegraphic Transfer (TT) fees are passed on to the client — most are around USD $20.

Bank wire withdrawals usually take three to five working days to reach your account.

FAQ

Funding questions

How do I add funds to my trading account?
Log in to your account with your username and password. Inside the client portal, open the Funds section, choose from the available payment methods, enter the amount and confirm — your deposit is credited in just a few simple steps.
Do you charge any fees for payments or withdrawals?
We do not charge any fees for depositing funds to your account, and we do not charge any fees for withdrawals in most countries.
How do I withdraw funds?
Log in to your account, open the Withdrawal section of the client portal, select your preferred method, enter the amount and submit the request — our team then processes it in line with the timeframes above.
