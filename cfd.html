FAQ

CFD trading questions

What is a CFD?
A contract for difference is an agreement to exchange the difference in an asset’s price between opening and closing a position. You never own the underlying asset, which is what lets you go short as easily as long.
What can I trade as a CFD?
Forex pairs, spot metals and energies, global indices and individual share CFDs — all from the same account balance and the same platform.
How does leverage work on CFDs?
Leverage lets you control a larger position than your deposit alone would allow, up to 1:1000 on eligible accounts. It scales the loss exactly as far as it scales the gain, so size the position before you size the leverage.
Are there overnight costs?
Positions held overnight are subject to a financing adjustment, credited or debited depending on the instrument and direction. Spot metals and indices carry no expiry, so the position rolls rather than settles.
Can I go short?
Yes. A CFD is a two-way instrument, so selling is as straightforward as buying and needs no borrowing arrangement.
What is a CFD broker?

A CFD broker, also known as a Contracts for Difference broker, refers to an intermediary that helps in the trading of contracts for difference, which are financial instruments that enable you to bet on the price movement of an asset without having to own the asset itself. As you look for an online CFD broker, it is essential that you look at the regulation, the markets, the platform, as well as the fees and spreads.

What is a CFD trading platform?

A CFD trading platform refers to the software that you use in the trading of your contracts for difference, including the analysis of charts as well as the monitoring of your accounts. Some of the most popular CFD trading platforms include the MT4 and the MT5. The quality of your CFD trading software plays an important role in ensuring that you trade efficiently.

How do I open a CFD account?

Generally speaking, the process of opening a CFD brokerage account takes about three steps: filling out the online registration form, verifying your identification using the necessary documents, and depositing money into the account. Most brokerage companies will walk you through each step of the way.

What can I trade with a CFD account?

If you are still unsure about the brokerage platform and wish to test the waters before risking your hard-earned money, then the demo mode is the way to go. Once you are comfortable with the way the system operates, then you can switch to the live mode.

A typical CFD brokerage account offers access to a wide variety of markets from one single platform. Most accounts allow access to currency pairs, stock markets from across the globe, stocks from individual companies, as well as commodities such as gold, oil, and agricultural products.

What should I look for in a CFD trading platform?

When evaluating CFD trading platforms, the main aspects to consider are the speed of execution, the charts, the types of orders, and the compatibility with different devices. The platform should enable you to effectively analyze the market situation, as well as execute your trading decisions without delay. The reliability of the platform is equally important, as a slow platform during market fluctuations may cause you to lose money.

What is the best platform for CFD trading?

The best CFD trading platform is the one that best matches your needs as a trader. MT4 is an excellent choice if you are a CFD trader looking for a powerful, lightweight platform with wide community support. MT5 is the best choice if you are a CFD trader looking for extra time frames, types of orders, as well as an economic calendar. In addition, some CFD brokers provide their own CFD online trading platforms, which are referred to as online CFD trading platforms.

What trading tools should a CFD platform offer?

Effective tools in CFD trading enable you to trade more efficiently. The basic tools that you should look for in the trading platform include advanced charts with various time frames, drawing tools, technical indicators, an economic calendar, and news feeds. Risk management tools, including stop-loss and take-profit orders, are also necessary tools in CFD trading.

Is online CFD trading suitable for beginners?

CFD trading online can be suitable for beginners if you are willing to learn the basics of contracts for difference, understand the risks involved, and develop a structured approach to trading. It would be best if you also had the experience of trading through a demo account, as it would be the best way to get started in CFD trading. There are also learning tools that you can use, including those that teach you the basics of CFD trading, as well as more advanced tools that would be helpful in your trading activities.
