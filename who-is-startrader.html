Who is STARTRADER?

STARTRADER is a Forex and CFD broker that provides traders and partners with access to global financial markets through trading platforms, tools, and support services. STARTRADER aims to make global trading accessible, trusted, and empowering while providing a flexible trading environment.

What Does STARTRADER Offer?

STARTRADER provides access to global financial markets and supports traders through:

  • Trading platforms and infrastructure
  • Market access and trading tools
  • Flexible trading solutions
  • Multilingual customer support
  • A regulatory framework across multiple jurisdictions

Available products, services, and account features may vary depending on your location and the STARTRADER entity under which your account is registered.

Our Vision

STARTRADER’s vision is to make global trading accessible, trusted, and empowering, helping people discover opportunities to grow their future.

Our Mission

STARTRADER’s mission is to empower traders and partners through transparent market access, advanced tools, and trusted support so they can participate in global financial markets with confidence.

Our Core Values

  • Strength: Strong platforms, infrastructure, and a regulatory framework help traders make clear and informed decisions.
  • Trust: Transparency, regulation, and integrity form the foundation of a reliable trading experience.
  • Achievement: Support and reliable solutions help clients achieve outstanding results and work towards their trading goals.
  • Resilience: Support and flexibility help clients navigate market movements and work towards their long-term objectives.

For more information about STARTRADER, visit the official STARTRADER website.

Risk Warning

CFDs are complex financial instruments and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You should ensure that you fully understand the risks involved and carefully consider whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money before trading.

RISK WARNING

Trading in leveraged derivative products, such as Foreign Exchange (Forex) and Contracts for Difference (CFDs), involves a high level of risk and may result in losses that exceed your initial deposit. These products may not be suitable for all investors. Leverage amplifies both potential profits and losses. When trading these instruments, you do not own or have any rights to the underlying assets. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Before making any investment decisions, you should carefully assess your investment objectives, knowledge, experience, and financial situation. You should only engage in Forex and CFD trading if you possess sufficient knowledge and experience. STARTRADER assumes no liability for any losses incurred from trading, and all trading activities are undertaken at your own risk.

The content provided on this website is for general informational purposes only and does not take into account your individual financial circumstances, investment goals, or risk tolerance. You should review the legal documents available on our website to ensure a full understanding of the risks associated with CFD trading. If necessary, seek independent advice from qualified professionals before proceeding.
