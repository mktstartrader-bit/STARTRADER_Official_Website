FAQ

Financial Commission questions

What is the Financial Commission?
The Financial Commission (FINACOM PLC LTD. and affiliates) is an independent self-regulatory organisation and external dispute resolution (EDR) body dedicated specifically to Forex. It resolves disputes between traders and member brokers and provides education about Forex and CFDs.
How much compensation can a case receive?
Membership gives clients access to compensation of up to EUR 20,000 per case, paid from the Financial Commission's compensation fund.
Should I contact the Financial Commission first?
No. Raise the matter with STARTRADER first by emailing support@startrader.com with Complaint in the subject line. Most issues are resolved directly. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can then take the case to the Financial Commission.
Is the Financial Commission the same as a regulator?
No. It is a self-regulatory organisation and dispute resolution body, not a financial regulator. Each STARTRADER entity is separately authorised and supervised by its own regulator, and those licences are listed on the regulation page.
How are cases decided?
The Financial Commission's stated aim is that disputes between traders and brokers are resolved in a quick, efficient, unbiased and authentic manner, independently of the broker involved.
Does it cover trading losses?
No. Dispute resolution addresses how a broker handled your account, not the outcome of your trades. Leveraged products carry a high level of risk and can result in losses that exceed your initial deposit.
