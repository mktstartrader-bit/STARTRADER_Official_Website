FAQ

Agricultural trading questions

Which agricultural instruments can I trade?
Wheat, corn and soybean on the grain side, and coffee, sugar and cocoa on the softs side — six CFDs priced off the underlying futures.
What drives agricultural prices most?
Growing-region weather first, then harvest and stocks reports, then export policy in the major producing countries. Currency matters too — a weaker Brazilian real encourages selling.
Are these markets seasonal?
Yes. Planting and harvest windows create a recurring pattern in both price and volatility, though a weather shock will override the seasonal picture entirely.
Do I need to roll a contract?
No. The CFD tracks the underlying futures without requiring you to manage the expiry yourself.
Can I go short?
Yes. Selling is as straightforward as buying, which lets you trade an expected bumper harvest as readily as a shortage.
