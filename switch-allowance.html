FAQ

About the switch rewards

How is the 1% switch allowance worked out?
It is one per cent of the smaller of the two amounts — what you withdrew from your old broker and what you deposited with STARTRADER. Withdraw $2,000 and deposit $1,000 and the allowance is $10; withdraw $500 and deposit $1,000 and it is $5.
Is there a cap on the switch allowance?
No. There is no maximum limit on that reward, and it is applied instantly once your withdrawal proof is verified.
How much cashback can I earn?
STP accounts earn $5 per lot traded and ECN accounts $2 per lot, each capped at $200. The earning period runs for 60 days from your first deposit, on deposits of $3,000 or more.
What proof do I need to send?
The withdrawal confirmation from your previous broker. Send it to the support team by email or live chat and they will confirm once it is verified.
