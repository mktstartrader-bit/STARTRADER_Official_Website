What is an Introducing Broker (IB) Program?

An Introducing Broker (IB) program is a partnership model in which an individual or business refers new traders to a brokerage and earns rebates or commissions based on their clients' trading activity. Unlike affiliate programs that pay a one-time fee per referral, an IB partnership provides ongoing revenue - you continue to earn as long as your referred clients are actively trading.

STARTRADER Introducing Broker Program FAQs

What Is an Introducing Broker (IB) Program?

An Introducing Broker (IB) program is a partnership arrangement where an individual or business refers new traders to a brokerage. In return, the IB earns ongoing rebates or commissions based on the trading volume generated by their referred clients. Unlike a one-time affiliate payout, IB earnings are recurring - you continue to earn for as long as your clients trade.

Who Can Become a STARTRADER Introducing Broker Partner?

The STARTRADER Introducing Broker Program is open to a wide range of partners, including affiliate marketers, trading educators, signal providers, financial professionals, social media influencers, and anyone with a network of potential traders. No prior brokerage experience is required - STARTRADER provides onboarding support and resources to help you get started.

How Does the STARTRADER Introducing Broker Program Work?

Once approved as a STARTRADER IB, you receive a unique referral link to share with your audience. When someone registers through your link and begins trading, you earn a rebate on every lot they trade. You can manage your referral activity, track client performance, and monitor your earnings through the STARTRADER IB Portal.

How Much Can a STARTRADER Introducing Broker Earn?

Your earnings depend on the trading volume of your referred clients. STARTRADER's IB rebate model has no earning cap - the more clients you refer and the more actively they trade, the greater your income. Rebates are calculated on a per-lot basis across forex and CFD products.

When and How Are IB Commissions Paid?

STARTRADER processes IB rebate payments on a regular schedule. Payouts are available through multiple withdrawal methods, giving you flexibility in how and when you receive your earnings. You can track all payout details in real time via the IB Portal.

How Do I Access the IB Portal?

After your IB application is approved, you will receive login credentials for the STARTRADER IB Portal. The portal is accessible via web browser and provides tools for real-time commission tracking, client activity monitoring, referral link generation, and performance analytics.

Is There Any Cost to Join STARTRADER's Introducing Broker Program?

No. Joining the STARTRADER IB Program is completely free. There are no registration fees, monthly charges, or hidden costs. Simply complete the application form, and once approved, you can start referring clients and earning rebates.
