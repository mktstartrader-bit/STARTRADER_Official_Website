How It Works

  1. i. Eligible clients must make a qualifying deposit of at least USD 100 into their STARcopy account and complete at least 1 standard lot in copy trade in qualifying closed trades, both within the same Weekly Qualifying Window, to receive a Cashback Voucher.

Week 1 (16–22 November 2026): The client deposits USD 100 into their STARcopy account, meeting the USD 100 minimum threshold. Trading Requirement

To unlock a Cashback Voucher for a given Weekly Qualifying Window, the client must complete at least 1 standard lot of copy trade in qualifying closed trades within that same window, in the following instruments: FX, Gold, Silver, Oil and BTC. Each qualifying position must remain open for at least ten minutes; positions held for less time will not count toward the requirement.
