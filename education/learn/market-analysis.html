Structure

Trend, range or transition — before anything else, what regime is price actually in?

  1. 01

    Structure first

    We start with the shape of the market, not the news. Higher highs or lower lows, an expanding range or a coiling one — the regime decides which tools are even valid, and it is the single thing most retail analysis skips.

  2. 02

    Then the catalyst

    A clean technical setup that runs into a rate decision is not a clean setup. We map the releases, the speakers and the auctions against the chart so you know which hours carry event risk and which are yours to trade.

  3. 03

    Finally the levels

    Every report ends with the two prices that matter: the level that confirms the read and the level that invalidates it. No open-ended targets — if we cannot say where the idea is wrong, we do not publish it.

FAQ

About the analysis

How often is the analysis published?
Technical and fundamental notes are published every trading day before the London open. The weekly outlook lands on Monday morning and the monthly review in the first week of each month.
Which markets does the desk cover?
Major and minor forex pairs, gold and silver, crude oil and natural gas, the major equity indices and a rotating list of single shares — the same instruments you can trade on the platform.
Is the analysis a trade recommendation?
No. Everything published here is general market commentary for information only. It does not take your objectives, financial situation or needs into account and is not a personal recommendation to buy or sell any instrument.
Do I need an account to read the reports?
No. The analysis is free to read without an account. Opening a live or demo account simply lets you act on what you read on the same platform.
Where do the levels come from?
From market structure on the chart — prior swing points, session highs and lows, and areas where price has repeatedly reacted. They are marked so you can judge them yourself rather than take them on trust.
