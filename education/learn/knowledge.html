FAQ

Getting started

Where should a complete beginner start?
Start at the Basics level and read the forex guide first — it explains what you are actually buying and selling. From there, currency pairs and pips, then risk management before you place a live trade.
Do I need an account to use the Knowledge Centre?
No. Every guide, video and glossary entry is free to read without an account. A demo account is useful for practising what you learn on live pricing with virtual funds.
How long does each guide take?
Most guides are between four and ten minutes. Each card shows its reading time so you can pick something that fits the gap you have.
Is the material specific to STARTRADER?
The concepts apply to trading anywhere. Where a guide covers a platform feature — an order type or a chart tool — it uses MT4, MT5 and the STAR platforms, because those are the ones you will have.
Will learning this make me profitable?
No education can promise that. Leveraged products carry a high level of risk and can cost more than your initial deposit. What this material can do is make sure your decisions are informed ones and that you understand the risk you are taking.
