FAQ

Black Friday Power Play FAQ

01

What is Black Friday Power Play?

It's a limited-time STARTRADER promotion where eligible clients can earn lucky draw tickets for 100 USD 20 credit bonuses and compete for four top prizes by meeting the required deposit and trading volume thresholds.

02

Who can join Black Friday Power Play?

The promotion is open to new and existing STARTRADER clients residing in eligible countries or regions, subject to the Terms & Conditions. Cent, STAR Copy, PAMM, and MAM accounts are excluded.

03

When does the promotion run?

From 23 November to 4 December 2026, based on server time.

04

How do I participate?

Opt in through the Client Portal or STARTRADER app, meet the applicable net deposit requirement, and trade eligible products. Only trading volume generated after opting in counts.

05

Which products and trades qualify?

Forex, Gold, Silver, Oil, and BTC are eligible. Only fully closed positions held for at least 10 minutes count toward qualifying trading volume.

06

How do I earn lucky draw tickets?

Maintain a minimum net deposit of USD 500 (or equivalent) and earn one ticket for every USD 1,000,000 in qualifying notional trading volume. There is no ticket limit.

07

How is notional trading volume calculated?

Notional volume is the USD value of your closed positions, calculated as:

Trading Volume (Lots) × Contract Size × Opening Price

Only complete USD 1,000,000 increments earn tickets. For example, USD 3,500,000 in qualifying volume earns three tickets.

08

What can I win in the lucky draw?

100 winners will each receive a USD 20 credit bonus. Winners are selected randomly, and each client can win only once. More tickets increase your chances but do not guarantee a prize.

09

Can I withdraw my credit bonus?

No. The USD 20 credit bonus is non-withdrawable and can only be used for trading. Trading profits may be withdrawn under standard withdrawal procedures. Withdrawals may result in the credit bonus being deducted.

10

How do I qualify for the top prizes?

Maintain a minimum net deposit of USD 1,000 and complete at least USD 2,000,000 in qualifying notional trading volume during the promotion.

11

How are the top four winners selected?

Qualified clients are ranked by total eligible notional trading volume. If two clients achieve the same volume, the client who reaches it first ranks higher.

12

What are the top prizes?

  • 1st Place: MacBook Air
  • 2nd Place: Leica D-Lux 8
  • 3rd Place: Nintendo Switch 2
  • 4th Place: Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2

Physical prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash. If a prize is unavailable or cannot be delivered, STARTRADER may substitute an item or credit of equivalent value.

13

Can I qualify for both reward categories?

Yes. Clients who qualify for the top prize ranking remain eligible for the lucky draw using their accumulated tickets.

14

How is my net deposit calculated, and what happens if I withdraw?

Net deposit equals total deposits minus withdrawals and outgoing internal transfers during the promotion. Credit bonuses, rebates, and incoming internal transfers are excluded.

Your net deposit must remain at or above USD 500 for lucky draw eligibility or USD 1,000 for top prize ranking eligibility until the promotion ends. Falling below the applicable threshold will result in disqualification from that reward category.

15

When will winners be announced?

Lucky draw winners and the final top prize rankings will be announced on 9 December 2026. Winners will also be notified by email.

16

How and when will prizes be delivered?

Lucky draw credit bonuses will be credited to participating trading accounts within 24 hours following the announcement.

Top prize winners will be contacted to arrange delivery and must provide their delivery details within 14 calendar days of notification to avoid potential forfeiture. Any applicable taxes, duties, or fees are the winner's responsibility.
