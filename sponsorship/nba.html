About the partnership

What does Official Partner of the NBA mean for clients?
It is a marketing partnership, not a trading product. What it changes for a client is reach and recognition: the same account, the same conditions, backed by a brand that now travels with one of the most-watched leagues in the world.
Why basketball?
Both the league and the market reward preparation, repetition and composure under pressure. The values line up more closely than the two industries suggest — which is the whole reason the partnership works.
Where can I get the wallpapers?

Does the partnership affect my trading costs?
No. Spreads, commissions and execution are set by your account type and by market conditions, and are unaffected by any sponsorship.
