What does the partnership involve?
STARTRADER is the Official Partner of the UAE National Cricket Team for the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2026. It is a brand partnership: it does not change account conditions, spreads or execution.
Why cricket?
Because the disciplines rhyme. A batter reads conditions before committing to a shot, and a trader reads a market before committing to a position. Both are judged over a season rather than a single ball.
Where can I follow the fixtures?
The group-stage dates, opponents and venues are listed on this page. Times and any changes are confirmed by the ICC closer to each match.
Does the sponsorship affect my trading costs?
No. Spreads, commissions and execution are set by your account type and by market conditions, and are unaffected by any sponsorship.
