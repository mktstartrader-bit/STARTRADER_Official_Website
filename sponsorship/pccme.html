FAQ

About the partnership

What is the Porsche Carrera Cup Middle East?
A one-make championship run to Porsche’s own regulations, with every driver in an identical 911 GT3 Cup. Rounds are held across the region, several of them alongside international race weekends.
What does STARTRADER’s partnership involve?
STARTRADER is an official partner of the championship and appears on car No. 7. It is a brand partnership — it does not change account conditions, spreads or execution.
Why does a broker sponsor motorsport?
Because the disciplines rhyme. Both reward preparation over prediction, both punish a late decision, and both are judged over a season rather than a session.

Pick any design in the rail on this page and download it, or take the full set of fifteen in one archive. They are free and there is nothing to sign up for.
