About EIEV

A Heritage of Endurance

Emirates International Endurance Village (EIEV) in Al Wathba, Abu Dhabi, is dedicated to endurance riding, a sport rooted in Arabian equestrian heritage.

The sport is built on a partnership between horse and rider that develops over time. Together, they pace each ride carefully and adapt as it develops, while veterinary checks throughout the race keep horse welfare at the centre of the competition.

Our sponsorship

Shared Values. Lasting Commitment.

Why We Sponsor EIEV Pulse

Our sponsorship for the 2026–2027 season is based on the values behind our name: Strength, Trust, Ambition and Resilience. These values guide our commitment to traders worldwide and apply equally to endurance riding.

01

Strength

In endurance racing, horses and riders build strength through conditioning before they compete. At STARTRADER, we build strength by investing in the technology behind our trading platforms.

02

Trust

The trust between horse and rider is one of the qualities we value in endurance racing. In our own work, we earn trust by being transparent about how our services work and providing consistent client support.

03

Ambition

Competitors aim to improve and take on new challenges in every race. We apply the same ambition to helping traders build their knowledge and access global markets.

04

Resilience

Endurance racing tests the ability of horse and rider to maintain a steady effort over long distances. At STARTRADER, we focus on keeping our trading tools reliable through periods of market volatility.
