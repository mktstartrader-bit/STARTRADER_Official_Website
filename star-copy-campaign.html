FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01What is STAR Copy?

STAR Copy is the copy trading feature on the STARTRADER web platform and app. It connects STAR Copiers with experienced STAR Masters, so a copier can mirror a strategy without running it by hand.

02What is the minimum to start using STAR Copy?

USD 50, in any account currency, with lots from 0.0001. There is no separate subscription.

03What can I trade with STAR Copy?

More than 1,000 instruments across forex, shares, indices, ETFs and commodities in both metals and energies.

04What are the steps to start copying?

Open a copy trading account on the web platform or in the app. Once it is verified and funded, pick the masters you want to follow, set the amount you are comfortable with, and monitor from the same screen.

05Can I cancel an order at any time?

Yes. Open the orders page and cancel the copied order. You can also pause or stop copying a master entirely, at any moment.

06Can I copy several masters at once?

Yes, as long as the account keeps a healthy margin. Spreading across masters can spread risk, but it does not guarantee a better result.

07How do I become a STAR Master?

Make a single deposit of at least USD 500 and apply from the app or the web platform. You are also expected to show trading experience and responsible risk management.

08Can I be a STAR Master and a STAR Copier at the same time?

Yes. One copy trading account can copy other masters and be copied itself.

09How does a STAR Master publish a strategy?

Either through the manual trading feature inside the copy trading account, or by syncing an MT4 or MT5 account through the Signal Provider Centre.

10Can I start / stop copying a STAR Master when the market is closed?

Yes, you can. Copiers can start and stop copying a STAR Master when the market is closed. However, open positions can only be closed after the market reopens. Before then, their status will display as "Pending Close".

11Are STAR Copiers able to close their trades manually?

Yes, but you can only close the entire position. Partial positions cannot be closed manually.

12What happens when a copy trade hits the “Stop Loss” percentage you’ve set?

All positions will be forced to close and you will stop following the STAR Master’s strategy. If your account balance reaches zero, any remaining credits will be forfeited.

b. The HWM is defined as the highest historical floating profit level achieved on the Copier’s account while copying from a specific Signal Provider. Signal Providers are only eligible to earn a performance fee if the current floating profit level exceeds the HWM at the time of settlement.

c. The floating profit level is evaluated and settled according to the applicable settlement period, as follows:

Daily: At 00:00 (UTC+0) every calendar day.

Weekly: At 00:00 (UTC+0) every Saturday.

Monthly: At 00:00 (UTC+0) on the first calendar day of each month.

All settlement times are based on UTC+0, irrespective of Daylight Saving Time or Standard Time. This schedule is subject to change without notice. The Company disclaims any liability for delays, calculation errors, or technical issues that may affect fee settlement, including but not limited to system outages, connectivity failures, or platform disruptions.

d. A Copier’s current floating profit level refers to the net result of all open (unrealised) and closed (realised) trading positions copied from a specific Signal Provider, as evaluated at each settlement under Clause 10.3(c). This profit level is compared against the previously established HWM to determine whether a performance fee is payable. If the current floating profit level exceeds HWM, the difference may be subject to the agreed profit-sharing ratio, and a performance fee will be paid to the Signal Provider. No fee is payable in the event of a net loss or if the floating profit level remains below HWM.
