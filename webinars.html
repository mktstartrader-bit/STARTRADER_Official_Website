FAQ

Webinar questions

Are STARTRADER webinars free to attend?
Yes. Every session is free, and you don’t need a funded trading account to join. You register with your name and email so we can send you the joining link and a reminder before it starts.
Do I need a STARTRADER account to join a webinar?
No. The sessions are open to anyone learning the markets. If you do hold a live or demo account you’ll get more out of the platform workshops, because you can follow along in MT4, MT5 or the STAR platforms while the presenter works through each step.
Which languages are the webinars presented in?
The language of each session is shown with its date and time, and again on the registration form, so you know before you sign up.
What if I can’t attend a live session?
Register anyway. We only run a few sessions a year, and registered attendees hear first when the next one is scheduled.
What level of experience do I need?
Each session carries a level tag — beginner, intermediate, advanced or all levels. Sessions are pitched so newcomers can follow, while topics such as Ichimoku and market sentiment assume you’re already comfortable reading a chart.
How long does a webinar last, and can I ask questions?
Most sessions run 45 to 60 minutes, including a live Q&A at the end. You can type questions at any point during the broadcast and the presenter answers as many as time allows — the Q&A is usually the most useful part of the hour.
What software do I need to join?
Nothing to install. Sessions stream in your browser on desktop, tablet or mobile — you only need a stable internet connection. The joining link arrives by email and works with a single tap.
Is a webinar investment advice?
No. Webinars are educational and general in nature. They don’t take account of your objectives, financial situation or needs, and nothing presented should be treated as a recommendation to trade a specific instrument. Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of losing money rapidly.
