FAQ

CPA Affiliate questions

When does a referred client qualify?
A client qualifies once they have deposited the tier minimum of $500 and traded 2 lots across FX, oil, gold or crypto. The CPA is then payable at your tier’s rate in the following month’s settlement.
How does the monthly bonus work?
The bonus is on top of your CPA and requires minimum net deposits of 2.5× your monthly CPA commission total — a 250% threshold. Bonuses reach up to $10,000 per month on Tiers 1 to 3. Exact terms are set out in the partnership agreement.
When am I paid?
Settlement is monthly, on the 15th, covering the previous month’s qualified activity. Payout methods are confirmed during onboarding.
What if my clients are outside the three tiers?
Clients from countries not listed in Tiers 1 to 3 carry a $100 CPA on the same 2-lot trading requirement, and the monthly bonus does not apply. If most of your traffic comes from one unlisted country, mention it on the form — terms are reviewed case by case.
Can I join if I already work with another broker?
Yes, and you should say so on the application — the partnerships desk asks about existing broker relationships as part of the review. It does not disqualify you.
Which promotion methods are allowed?
Affiliates typically use their own websites, content, paid search, social channels and video. Tell us which channels you plan to use on the form; the partnerships desk confirms what is permitted for your region, since financial promotion rules differ by jurisdiction.
