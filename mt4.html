FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the MT4 trading platform?
MetaTrader 4 is the trading platform most retail traders learned on. It offers real-time price charts and a deep set of indicators to help you read market trends, and with Expert Advisors it can execute trades automatically against rules you define in advance.
What are the key features of MT4 that benefit STARTRADER users?
Advanced charting across nine timeframes with 50+ technical indicators; 24 analytical tools for marking structure; one-click and chart trading; trailing stops; accessibility across desktop and mobile; Expert Advisors for automation; trading signals from top providers; and support for custom EAs and indicators of your own.
How do I connect my STARTRADER account to MT4?
Download and install MT4, open it and choose File → Login to Trade Account, then enter the account credentials and server details STARTRADER issued when you opened the account. Click Login and the platform connects.
Can I use Expert Advisors with my STARTRADER account?
Yes. EAs install and configure on the platform as normal, so you can automate a strategy against predetermined rules and conditions. Test one against historical data before you let it trade live money.
What are the system requirements?
The desktop terminal runs on Microsoft Windows 8, 10 and 11, and on macOS. A connection of 1 Mbps or better is comfortable. If you would rather not install anything, the browser version needs no download at all.
