FAQ

Who can join?

Clients residing in an Eligible Country. Referrers must be existing STARTRADER clients with an Active Account; Referees must be New Eligible Clients who have never previously registered with or held an account with STARTRADER.

How much can I earn?

USD 200 per Successful Referral, up to 5 referrals (maximum USD 1,000). Your friend receives USD 100.

What makes a referral successful?

Your friend must sign up using your referral link, complete identity verification within 48 hours, deposit a minimum of USD 100, and complete at least 5 Eligible Lots within 30 days, all during the Promotion Period.

Why do I need to add STARTRADER on LINE?

Both the Referrer and Referee must follow STARTRADER’s official LINE account to qualify. Your referral link and reward updates are also provided via LINE.

When do I get my reward?

Within 5 business days after your friend’s 30-day qualification period ends, subject to review.

What counts as an Eligible Lot?

A closed, round-turn trade (one standard lot) in FX, Gold, Crude Oil or BTC CFDs, with the position held for at least 10 minutes.

Can the promotion end early?

Yes. Once the USD 50,000 reward pool is fully allocated, the Promotion will close, even before 12 November.
