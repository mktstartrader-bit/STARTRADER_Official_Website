FAQ

For journalists

Where has STARTRADER been covered?
Across the industry titles that review brokers on execution and conditions — WikiFX, DailyForex, Myfxbook, FX Empire and Trading.de among them — as well as regional press on licensing and sponsorship news.
How do I request a comment or interview?
Contact the press desk through the contact page. Tell us the outlet, the angle and your deadline, and it will reach the right person rather than sitting in a general inbox.
Can I use your brand assets?
Logos and brand marks are available on request for editorial use. We ask that the mark is not altered, recoloured or placed on a background that changes its meaning.
Who speaks on behalf of STARTRADER?
Senior management and the research desk, depending on the subject. Market commentary comes from the desk; corporate matters come from management.
