FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is STAR Web Trading?
A browser-based platform that reaches global markets directly from a web browser. Nothing is installed. You get forex, indices, commodities and share CFDs with advanced charting, real-time quotes and the risk controls you would expect from the desktop terminal.
Do I need to download any software?
No. It is entirely browser-based, so you can open it on any compatible browser, sign in with your STARTRADER credentials and trade straight away. There is also no version to keep updated.
What markets can I trade?
Forex majors, minors and exotics; global indices such as US30, SPX500 and FTSE 100; commodities including gold, silver, oil and natural gas; and share CFDs. Every instrument carries real-time quotes and the full charting toolset.
What are the system requirements?
Any device with a current browser and a stable internet connection. Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari are all supported on desktops, laptops, tablets and phones. Performance varies with the device, the browser version and the stability of your network.
Can I use it on mobile devices?
Yes. The platform is responsive and carries every feature onto tablets and phones. For phones specifically the STARTRADER app is the more comfortable experience, but nothing is missing from the browser.
Is it the same account as the desktop platform?
Yes. Same login, same balance, same open positions — the browser is another window onto the account, not a separate one.
How do I contact support?
Use the online support icon inside the platform for live chat, or email info@startrader.com. Having your account number to hand makes it quicker.

Trading CFDs involves significant risk and is not suitable for every investor. Leverage works for you and against you in equal measure. Consider whether you understand how CFDs work, and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
