FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best forex platform for web trading?
The one that loads fast, quotes accurately and then gets out of the way. Web Trader carries the same execution, instrument list and analysis tools as the installed terminal, so the convenience of the browser costs you nothing in capability.
How do I choose a forex trading platform?
Match it to how you actually trade. Check the instruments it covers, the order types it accepts, the risk tools it gives you, and how orders are executed. Then open it on the device you will really be using, not the one you wish you were using.
What features should I look for in a trading platform?
A layout you can read under pressure, charting deep enough to test an idea, a support desk that answers, and education you can act on. Depth of drawing tools and the quality of the data matter more than the raw count of indicators.
Is web trading secure?
Sessions are encrypted in transit, accounts sit behind additional authentication where it is available, and client funds are held in segregated accounts. The remaining risk is on your side of the connection — use a network you trust and do not save credentials on a shared machine.
Can I trade forex on my mobile device?
Yes. Web Trader is responsive down to a phone, and the STARTRADER app is built for phones specifically. Both read the same account, so a position opened on one is managed from the other.
What is the difference between web and desktop platforms?
In function they are close to identical. The desktop terminal keeps working offline for chart study and holds heavier custom tooling such as expert advisors; the browser starts in seconds on any machine and needs nothing installed. Most traders end up using both.
