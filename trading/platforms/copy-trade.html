FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does copy trading cost?
You pay the normal trading costs on the positions copied into your account, plus a performance fee to the provider charged only on profit. There is no subscription and no fee in a losing period.
Can I stop copying at any time?
Yes. You can pause, resize or stop at any moment, and you can close individual copied positions yourself. The account remains yours throughout.
How do I choose a provider?
Look past the headline return. Maximum drawdown tells you what the strategy felt like at its worst, and the length of the history tells you whether the return is a method or a run of luck.
Can I still place my own trades?
Yes. Copied positions and your own can run on the same account side by side.
Is copy trading risk-free?
No. You are taking on the provider’s risk as well as market risk, and past performance says nothing certain about the future. Leveraged products can cost more than your initial deposit.
