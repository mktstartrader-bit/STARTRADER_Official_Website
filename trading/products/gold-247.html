FAQ

Gold 24/7 questions

Is XAUUSD247 a CFD?
Yes. As with standard gold CFDs, you take a position on the price of the underlying asset without owning it. The position may produce a profit or a loss depending on which way the price moves.
Do I own physical gold?
No. XAUUSD247 is a CFD product — traders do not own physical gold, they take positions on the price.
What does one lot represent?
One lot represents one ounce of gold. Position sizes move in single-ounce steps, from a minimum of one lot up to a maximum of 100 lots, or 100 ounces, per order.
Can I trade both XAUUSD and XAUUSD247?
Yes. You can trade both products as CFDs, or choose to trade only one — that choice depends on your preferences and overall approach.
