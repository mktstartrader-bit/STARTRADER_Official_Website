FAQ

Share CFD questions

What is a share CFD?
A contract on a company’s share price rather than the share itself. You take the price move in either direction without owning the stock, and without the settlement and custody that ownership brings.
Do I receive dividends?
You do not receive a dividend, but a cash adjustment is applied when the underlying goes ex-dividend — credited on a long position and debited on a short one.
Which shares can I trade?
More than a hundred names across US, European and Asian listings, weighted toward the most liquid large caps where the spread stays tight.
Do share CFDs have voting rights?
No. A CFD carries no shareholder rights, because there is no share in your name. If voting matters to you, an outright holding is the right instrument.
When can I trade them?
During the underlying exchange’s hours. US names quote through the US session, European names through the European session, and the spread widens near the open and the close.
