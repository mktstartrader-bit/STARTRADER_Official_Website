        STARTRADER CPA Affiliate Program FAQs

        • 1.  

          What is a Forex CPA affiliate program?

          A Forex CPA affiliate program allows you to earn a fixed commission for every referred client who completes a qualifying action with the broker. As a CPA affiliate partner, you promote the broker and receive a payout for each qualified trader you refer.

          2.  

          How Does the STARTRADER CPA Affiliate Program Work?

          You promote STARTRADER using your unique affiliate tracking links. When a referred client signs up and meets the qualification criteria, the referral is tracked in real time and a fixed CPA commission is credited to your affiliate account based on the applicable payout structure.

        • 3.  

          Who Can Become a STARTRADER CPA Affiliate Partner?

          The CPA affiliate program is open to individuals and businesses including media buyers, digital marketers, affiliate marketers, trading educators, content creators, finance communities and more looking to monetise trading traffic through performance-based commissions.

          4.  

          How Much Can I Earn As a Forex CPA Affiliate?

          Your earnings depend on the CPA payout per qualified client and the country tier of the referred trader. The more qualified referrals you generate, the higher your total monthly income potential.

        • 5.  

          When And How Are My Affiliate Payments Made?

          CPA commissions are paid on a scheduled basis (typically monthly) once referred clients are fully qualified. Payments are processed through multiple available withdrawal methods, ensuring reliable and consistent settlement cycles.

          6.  

          How Do I Track My CPA Performance?

          You can track all clicks, conversions, and commissions in real time through the STARTRADER affiliate portal. The dashboard provides transparent reporting so you can monitor performance and optimise your campaigns effectively.
