What is a PAMM Account

A PAMM (Percent Allocation Management Module) account allows retail traders to allocate trading capital to professional money managers. These managers trade pooled funds, and profits or losses are distributed proportionally based on each trader’s allocation.

How PAMM Accounts Work via Pro-Link

PAMM trading through Pro-Link is structured and transparent:

  1. Retail traders allocate capital to selected money managers
  2. Money managers execute trades using pooled funds
  3. Performance results are distributed proportionally
  4. Performance-based fees are applied when profits are generated
  5. All activity is managed within the STARTRADER trading environment.

Your capital stays in your own account but is combined with other traders' funds under a single master account for trading - you retain withdrawal rights and can reallocate between Pro-Link managers as market conditions evolve.

Why Choose Pro-Link for Your PAMM Account

Pro-Link is STARTRADER’s PAMM account system designed to connect retail traders with verified money managers in a transparent environment. Everything is designed to support informed trading decisions.

  • Verified and Monitored Money Managers

    Every money manager on Pro-Link is filtered by track record, risk level, and trading strategy, so you can compare before allocating capital.

  • Real-time Performance Tracking

    Follow your allocation's performance as it happens, with live updates on returns and account activity.

  • Transparent Allocation and Reporting System

    See exactly how your capital is allocated and how returns are calculated, with full reporting at every stage.

  • Built-in Risk Monitoring Tools

    Review risk metrics such as drawdown and historical volatility before choosing a money manager to trade with.

  • Performance-based Fee Structure

    Fees apply only when a money manager generates profit, aligning incentives between traders and managers.
