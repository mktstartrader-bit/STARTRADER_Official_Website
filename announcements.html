24Jun2026 Rollover

Rollover adjustment notice

Read notice

Dear valued client,

Futures-based CFDs on crude oil, natural gas and selected indices roll to the next contract month this week. Positions held through the roll are adjusted so that their economic value is unchanged: the price gap between contracts is offset by a cash adjustment to your account.

  • Roll window: 25–27 June 2026, at the close of each instrument’s session
  • Pending orders on affected instruments may be cancelled — please re-place them after the roll
  • Stop-loss and take-profit levels are not adjusted automatically; review them before the roll

For the full schedule per instrument, see the contract specifications in the platform.
