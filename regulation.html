Read this carefully

What a licence does — and doesn’t — mean

Being licensed is not the same as being endorsed, and not every entity above can do the same things. Both distinctions matter more than a logo wall.

A licence is authorisation, not endorsement

While the relevant authorities have granted securities or investment dealer licences to the licensees, they do not endorse the products or services offered by STARTRADER. Supervision covers the activities named in the licence.

Scope differs by entity

The UAE entity is licensed for Introduction and Promotion only and cannot execute trades. The Cyprus company is registered but offers no regulated products. Read the entity card before you assume what it covers.

Your entity depends on where you live

You contract with one entity, named in your client agreement and on your statements, and its regulator is the one you would approach with a complaint. Support will confirm which entity holds your account.

Regulation does not remove market risk

Leveraged products carry a high level of risk and can cost more than your initial deposit whichever entity you trade with. Oversight governs conduct; it does not cap what a position can lose.

Risk warning

Trading in leveraged derivative products, such as Foreign Exchange (Forex) and Contracts for Difference (CFDs), involves a high level of risk and may result in losses that exceed your initial deposit.

  • These products may not be suitable for all investors.
  • Leverage amplifies both potential profits and potential losses.
  • You hold no ownership rights over the underlying assets.
  • Past performance is not indicative of future results.
  • STARTRADER assumes no liability for any losses incurred from trading.

FAQ

Questions about our licensing

Which STARTRADER entity holds my account?
The entity is named in the client agreement you accepted when you registered, and on your account statements. Each entity, its regulator and its licence number are listed above. If you’re not sure which one applies to you, contact support and they’ll confirm it.
How do I check that a licence is genuine?
Take the licence number from this page and search for it on the regulator’s own public register rather than trusting a number printed on any website — including ours. Each entity card links to the regulator that issued its licence. If a number you’ve been shown elsewhere doesn’t appear on the register, treat it as unverified.
Does a licence mean the regulator endorses STARTRADER?
No. While the relevant authorities have granted securities or investment dealer licences to the licensees, they do not endorse the products or services offered by STARTRADER. A licence means the entity is authorised and supervised for the activities set out in it — nothing more.
What does the UAE entity’s licence actually permit?
STARTRADER Global Financial Consultation & Financial Analysis L.L.C is a Category 5 licensee authorised to carry out the regulated activities of Introduction and Promotion in the UAE. It is not authorised to provide brokerage services or execute client trades.
Is STARTRADER (CY) Limited a regulated broker?
No. STARTRADER (CY) Limited is registered in the Republic of Cyprus under registration number HE421001. It does not offer regulated financial products or provide trading services. It’s listed here for completeness because it carries the STARTRADER name.
Can I open an account from any country?
No. This website is not intended for distribution to, or use by, any person or entity who is a citizen or resident of certain jurisdictions, including but not limited to the United States, and jurisdictions listed on the FATF “blacklist” or other major global sanctions lists.
How do I raise a regulatory or compliance concern?
Email support@startrader.com with “Complaint” in the subject line and include your account number. The concern is logged and escalated to the compliance team of the entity your account is registered with, and you’ll receive written acknowledgement. If you’re not satisfied with the outcome, you can take the matter to the regulator named above for that entity.
Where are the registered offices?
Dubai, Sydney, Cape Town, Ebene Cybercity, Mahe and Limassol — every address is listed with its entity above. They’re corporate premises rather than retail branches, so account servicing is handled online or over chat.
