FAQ

Forex trading questions

What is forex trading and how does it work?
Forex trading is the buying and selling of currency pairs to profit from changes in their exchange rate. Trades use lots — a standard lot is 100,000 units, a mini lot 10,000 and a micro lot 1,000 — and leverage lets you control a larger position with less capital on the 24/5 foreign-exchange market.
How many currency pairs can I trade with STARTRADER?
You can trade 80+ currency pairs across major, minor and exotic categories, all with competitive spreads and fast execution on MT4, MT5 and the STAR platforms.
What are the trading hours for the forex market?
The forex market runs 24 hours a day, 5 days a week, closing only on weekends. Sessions overlap across Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York, so there is always an active market to trade.
What leverage is available on forex?
Flexible leverage is available up to 1:1000. Higher leverage increases both potential profit and risk, and leverage above 1:30 may not be available in some regions.
Which platforms can I use to trade forex?
MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 are the most popular, alongside the STAR Web Trader and our mobile apps — all offering advanced charting, customisable indicators and expert advisors to automate trades.
What are the best forex pairs to trade?
It depends on your strategy, but the most-traded pairs include EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, AUD/USD and USD/CAD — highly liquid majors with tight spreads and generally lower volatility than exotics.
Are there commissions or hidden fees on forex?
STARTRADER charges 0 commission and 0 deposit fees on most channels for Standard accounts. ECN and Prime ECN accounts pay a low commission in exchange for raw spreads from 0.0 pips.
How do I get started with forex trading?
Open an account, verify your identity, fund your wallet and start trading — or practise risk-free on a demo account first to build and test your strategy before going live.
